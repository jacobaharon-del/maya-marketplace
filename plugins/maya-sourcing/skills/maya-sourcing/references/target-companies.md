# Target company bank

The team's list of companies to source from. When a recruiter picks **Target
companies** in Maya's intake, she reads this file, pulls the relevant names
(filtered by category when the role calls for it), and pastes the whole list
into the LinkedIn Recruiter Companies filter in one shot (see
`linkedin-recruiter.md` §1b).

Anyone on the team can add companies here directly — just keep the
`## Category` structure so Maya can filter by category when a role calls for
it. A company with no clear category goes under `## General`. A company can
appear under more than one category.

Ported from the old Notion "Target Company Bank" database (245 companies).
